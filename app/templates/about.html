About Us

CorteX is committed to reducing food waste and addressing hunger. Our platform connects donors, NGOs, and beneficiaries to create a meaningful impact on society, enabling a future where no meal goes to waste, and no one goes hungry.

Our Vision

To create a world where no meal goes to waste, and no one goes hungry. By bridging the gap between surplus food and those in need, we aim to reduce food waste and empower communities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make food redistribution easy, transparent, and impactful. We provide a platform where restaurants, NGOs, and individuals can collaborate to reduce food waste and support the underprivileged. And the best part? It's **100% free**. No commissions, no fees—just giving.

Why CorteX?

CorteX stands out as a platform that not only combats food waste but also offers a simple, easy, and transparent way for people to make a real difference. Our platform connects you with those who need it the most, making the world a little bit better, one meal at a time.
