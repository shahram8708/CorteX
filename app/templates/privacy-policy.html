Privacy Policy

At CorteX, your privacy is a top priority. We are committed to safeguarding your personal information and ensuring a transparent, secure experience while using our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our platform, we collect personal information, including your name, email address, phone number, and food donation details. This helps us facilitate communication and effectively match donors with recipients.

2. Use of Information

The personal information you provide is used exclusively for the purpose of facilitating food donations, communication between users, and improving platform-related activities. We will never use your information for unsolicited purposes.

3. Data Security

We use industry-standard security protocols to protect your personal data. While we strive to secure your information to the best of our ability, please note that no method of transmission over the internet can guarantee 100% security.

4. Sharing of Information

We will never share your personal information with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. However, we may share it with trusted partners or service providers to fulfill platform-related activities or when legally required.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time. If you wish to exercise any of your rights or have questions about your personal data, please contact us at support@CorteX.com.

6. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally to reflect any changes to our platform or services. Any updates will be posted on this page with the revision date so that you are always aware of how we are safeguarding your personal information.
